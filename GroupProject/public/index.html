Deck Of Stories - Planning Poker

Estimate together, without the guesswork.

Deck Of Stories makes planning poker simple for agile teams, whether you’re in the same room or spread across time zones. Create a room, share the link, and everyone votes on story points at the same time, so no one’s estimate gets swayed by the loudest voice. Cards are revealed together, which shows right away where the team agrees and where it needs to talk. You spend less time arguing over numbers and more time building. No downloads and no setup, just faster and fairer sprint planning.
